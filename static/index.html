PickwiseShopping Agent

Demo: products come from outside retailers, but payment goes to this project's own PayPal sandbox merchant account. Orders are a concierge-style simulation: no real money moves and nothing ships.

What are you shopping for?

Describe it in one sentence, with a budget. You approve every payment yourself.

Orders

Your latest orders and their status. A refund is drafted in the chat and is sent only when you click Confirm refund.

What I remember

Lasting preferences the agent uses as soft hints when it ranks products. Delete any you do not want kept.
