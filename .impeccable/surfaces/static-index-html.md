## Direction contract

THESIS: A shopping assistant that feels like a luxury store's own site: quiet white space, black type, hairlines, and product tiles on a stone backdrop, so prices and the pay step read as calm and certain. It refuses chat-app chrome: tinted bubbles, rounded pills, saturated fills, icon logos.

OWN-WORLD: Quiet luxury in sand and espresso (owner's pick from the blend comparison, 2026-11-12): white main area, #f5f2ee sand sidebar, notice strip and product backdrops, #141414 ink and black buttons, #e8e2da hairlines, espresso #7a5a43 only for highlights (the shopper's message tint #f1eae2, the top pick label, the chosen product, the current view's icon, the agent's name). Near-square 4-6 px corners. Small uppercase tracked labels on card titles, ranks, the Ruled out toggle and the descriptor; controls in sentence case. Mark: three stacked cards, the front one checked, in espresso, beside the PICKWISE wordmark and the agent's name. Colour otherwise only for state. System fonts only.

STORY: The visitor reads the store's terms in the sand notice strip, asks in one sentence, sees three picks as a product grid with reasons and what was ruled out, then a bag-style summary whose total and single black button make the payment step unmistakable.
